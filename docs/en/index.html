Catch crop stress before it turns into yield loss

VigIA BioEstrés tracks every management zone from before planting and alerts you on WhatsApp days before water and heat stress shows up in the field. For growers and agronomists who make data-driven decisions.

Contact us to get started with VigIA

The problem

By the time the crop shows stress, the yield is already lost

When you see it in the field, you're reacting too late and the damage is done.

  • Symptoms show up too late for biostimulants

    Once leaves start rolling, yield is already compromised and a biostimulant does little to protect it.

  • A regional forecast doesn't know your field

    It can't tell how much water is left in your soil or what stage your crop is at.

No alert · damage shows in the field

What VigIA does

VigIA BioEstrés watches for stress all season long

It tracks every management zone from before planting to the end of the season, and tells you when to save water and when to apply a biostimulant.

  • Spots stress peaks

    A water shortage, dry air pulling water from the plant, or both at once. Alerts per management zone, with lead time.

  • From pre-planting

    Tracks soil water from before planting and alerts you 14 days ahead of the critical stage.

  • Smarter water use

    Know how much plant-available water each management zone has. When a shortage is coming, irrigate right when it is needed.

  • Built for biostimulants

    Every alert comes with its biostimulant: drought-stress, heat-stress, or preventive ahead of the critical stage.

  • Growing cover crops? VigIA also tracks their water use against fallow and tells you when to terminate them.

  • Used residual herbicides? With herbicide carryover tracking, you know when it is safe to plant the next crop.

The solution

Alerts in time to act

VigIA tracks every management zone, every day. When stress is coming, it alerts you with enough lead time to apply a biostimulant or irrigate.

  • On WhatsApp

    The alert reaches your phone with a suggested action, no need to log in anywhere.

  • On the platform

    The field dashboard, with the water balance and VPD behind every alert.

How it works

  1. Your fields are set up

    Management zones, soil and crop. Weather data connects automatically.

  2. Daily calculations

    Water balance and VPD against your crop thresholds.

  3. Timely alerts

    Log rainfall and irrigation, and the calculation adjusts.

With the alert · applied in time

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need sensors or weather stations in the field?

No. VigIA pulls weather data for the coordinates of each field. If you measure rainfall or soil water, you can log it and the calculation adjusts.

What do I need to enter?

At the start, your fields, management zones and soil are set up. After that, you just log rainfall and irrigation as they happen.

Which crops does it cover?

The main season crops (soybean development is adjusted for latitude and maturity group) and cover crops: cereal rye, barley and vetch.

How do I get the alerts?

On the platform, in each field dashboard, and on WhatsApp, without having to log in.

Does it work in rainfed fields?

Yes. In rainfed fields it alerts you in time to apply a biostimulant. Under irrigation, it also builds a monthly irrigation plan with its cost.
